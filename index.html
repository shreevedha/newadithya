Centers of Excellence

Specialized Clinical Services

Delivering high-end diagnostic, therapeutic & surgical care across all major clinical disciplines.

Cardiology & Cardiac Surgery

  • State-of-the-art Flat-Panel Cath Lab operating 24/7 for primary angioplasties.
  • Minimally Invasive Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG).
  • Advanced electrophysiology studies and dual-chamber pacemaker implantations.
  • Emergency response protocols for acute myocardial infarction (heart attack).
  • Complex valvular repair and congenital heart defect corrections.
  • Non-invasive diagnostic suite including color Doppler, Echo, and Holter monitoring.

Neurology & Neurosurgery

  • Comprehensive Stroke Care Center equipped with thrombolysis facilities.
  • Micro-neurosurgery for brain tumor excisions, spinal reconstructions, and aneurysms.
  • Dedicated Neuro Intensive Care Unit (NICU) with continuous neuromonitoring.
  • Advanced surgical and medical treatments for epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, and tremors.
  • Spinal surgery for herniated discs, spinal stenosis, and spinal cord injuries.
  • Electromyography (EMG) and Electroencephalography (EEG) diagnostic services.

Orthopaedics & Joint Reconstruction

  • MISSO Robotic Knee and Hip replacements for sub-millimeter precision.
  • Arthroscopic ligament reconstructions (ACL, PCL) and sports medicine.
  • Advanced management of complex fractures, polytrauma, and non-unions.
  • Minimally invasive spine surgeries and scoliosis corrections.
  • Specialized pediatric orthopaedics and bone deformity corrections.
  • Dedicated rehabilitation clinic for post-operative physical therapy.

Advanced Diagnostics & Radiology

  • High-resolution 3T Siemens MRI scanner for detailed structural imaging.
  • 128-Slice ultra-fast CT Scanner for coronary angiography and trauma screening.
  • High-definition ultrasound units with 3D/4D fetal imaging capabilities.
  • Digital mammography and dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) bone density scans.
  • Comprehensive pathology lab with automated clinical biochemistry and hematology.
  • Interventional radiology suite for non-surgical vascular treatments.
