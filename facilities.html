Infrastructure

World-Class Hospital Facilities

State-of-the-art diagnostic, critical care & surgical infrastructure engineered for patient recovery.

100-Bed Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Dedicated Medical ICU, Surgical ICU, Neuro ICU & Neonatal ICU (NICU) with HEPA air filtration, multipara monitors and 1:1 specialized nursing care.

Flat-Panel Digital Cath Lab

Advanced cardiac catheterization laboratory operating 24/7 for primary emergency angioplasty, pacemaker implantation & vascular interventions.

8 Modular Operation Theatres

Ultra-clean laminar airflow OTs equipped with C-Arm fluoroscopy, anti-bacterial paneling, and MISSO Robotic Joint replacement technology.

3T MRI & 128-Slice CT Imaging

High-resolution diagnostic radiology for neurological, cardiac, abdominal, vascular, and musculoskeletal imaging.

Advanced Endoscopy & GI Suite

HD video endoscopy units for diagnostic upper GI endoscopy, colonoscopy, ERCP, and video bronchoscopy.

24/7 In-House Pharmacy & Blood Storage

Fully stocked 24/7 pharmacy, automated pathology lab testing, and blood storage facility for instant clinical emergency support.
